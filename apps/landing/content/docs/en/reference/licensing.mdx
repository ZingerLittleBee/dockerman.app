Local features are free forever. Activate, manage, and reset a license when you need paid remote features.

## Overview

Local Docker and Podman features are free forever and do not require a license. A paid lifetime license unlocks remote hosts over SSH, multi-host management, Cloudflared tunnels, and Kubernetes cluster management. Dockerman prompts you to activate a license only when you use one of these paid features. See [Pricing](/en/pricing) for current plans.

## Activate

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open Settings → License

    Paste your license key into the License settings page.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Verify online

    Dockerman contacts the license server to verify the key.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Confirm activation

    The status changes to Active, and Dockerman stores the license key and device identifier locally.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage devices

Each activation is bound to a device identifier. If you replace a machine, lose access to one, or reach the device limit, open [License Self-Service](https://license.dockerman.app/manage) and enter the purchase email and license key. After you confirm the email, the service deactivates every device linked to the license so you can activate it again. The confirmation link expires after 30 minutes.

## Offline verification

After activation, Dockerman verifies the license locally on every launch with an Ed25519 signature. Day-to-day use does not require network access.

## Troubleshooting

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Activation fails with network error">
    Check your firewall and proxy settings, then try the activation again from Settings → License. If the error persists, use [License Self-Service](https://license.dockerman.app/manage).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Device limit reached">
    Use [License Self-Service](https://license.dockerman.app/manage) to deactivate all devices, then activate the license again on the machines you still use.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="License invalid after an update">
    Try reactivating the license first. If that does not help, email [support@dockerman.app](mailto:support@dockerman.app) and attach the App Log export.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
