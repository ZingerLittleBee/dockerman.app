---
title: Alert Rules
description: Enable preset alerts, choose channels, and review recent firings.
---

The Alerts page currently manages two built-in presets:

- **Restart loop**, triggered by a restart burst.
- **Container crash**, triggered by a non-zero container exit.

For each preset you can enable or disable it, choose additional notification channels, and reset it to defaults. The system notification channel remains mandatory.

## Current limitation

The **Create rule** button is disabled and marked Coming Soon. The desktop UI does not yet provide the documented custom-rule editor, target selector, severity editor, silence windows, or generic cooldown form. Do not plan workflows around creating health or resource-threshold rules in the current release.

## Recent alerts

The lower panel shows recent firings with rule name, a short context summary, and time. It is a compact feed, not the previously documented searchable, column-persistent data table.
