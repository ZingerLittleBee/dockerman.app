---
title: Stats
description: Live and historical resource charts for containers.
---

Open Stats from a container to view its CPU, memory, network, disk, and process data. The Stats route expects a selected container; it is not a host-wide overview.

## Time ranges

- Live: 1 minute, 5 minutes, or 15 minutes.
- Historical: 1 hour, 6 hours, 24 hours, or 7 days.

Historical data depends on the stats aggregation and retention tasks. Raw samples are retained for 1 hour and five-minute aggregates for 7 days.

## Charts

CPU and memory cards use ring gauges and history charts; network and disk cards show rates and expandable details. Ring colors are visual presentation, not documented alert thresholds.

## Compare

Enable Compare to select up to six host/container pairs. Compare uses historical aggregates and disables live mode. The selection persists locally.

<Callout type="info">
  Empty historical ranges mean no samples were recorded for that host and container during the window; they are not reconstructed from Docker after the fact.
</Callout>
